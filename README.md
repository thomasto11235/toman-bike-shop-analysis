# Toman Bike Shop Analysis
A beginner's data analysis project using SQL Server and Power BI.

---

## Overview
This project explores the business performance of Toman Bike Shop
through data cleaning, KPI calculation, and interactive visualization.

---

## Tools Used

### SQL Server
Used for data cleaning and calculation of key business KPIs:
- Ridership
- Revenue
- Profit
- Net Profit Margin
- Monthly Difference in Revenue
- 3-Day Moving Average

### Power BI
Used for data visualization to communicate business insights
to stakeholders. Key questions explored:
- Does the year-over-year price increase affect ridership
  and financial performance?
- Is there a correlation between environmental factors
  and monthly ridership?
- In which months does revenue typically drop and why?
- Which month saw the highest ridership and why?

---

## Key Insights

### 1. Price Elasticity
Despite a **25% year-over-year price increase**, ridership still
maintained substantial growth of **64%**.

This suggests that a moderate price increase of **10–15%** should
not significantly affect ridership, as indicated by a price
elasticity of **+2.56**.

### 2. Environmental Factors and Ridership
A correlation between ridership and environmental conditions was
examined. Key findings:
- Most riders were recorded during months with **low to medium humidity**
- A considerable number of riders still rode during higher humidity
  months, provided that **windspeed remained at an ideal level**

### 3. Revenue Trends
Revenue grew steadily and reached its peak in:
- **April 2021**
- **March 2022**

However, revenue dropped consistently during **October to December**
in both years, likely due to unfavorable environmental conditions
mentioned above.

## Recommendations

### 1. Market Analysis
Conduct further market research to better understand:
- Customer needs and satisfaction levels
- Potential competitive landscape changes
- Overall economic environment

This analysis can serve as a foundation for making informed
pricing decisions going forward.

### 2. Revenue Strategy
Develop strategies to address seasonal revenue drops by:
- Finding ways to attract customers during **favorable weather months**
  earlier in the year
- Evaluating alternative approaches to **retain ridership** during
  later months with poor weather conditions
- Properly advising customers on service usage during
  **adverse weather periods**
